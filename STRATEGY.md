## 1. Positioning

The AI-insider leaker persona is a proven format on X (the source account has ~44.9K posts
and strong engagement). We build a NEW account with the same voice DNA but an original handle
and no copy-paste of existing posts. The voice is the product: breathless insider, half
tease, half prophecy, lowercase, fragments, no hashtags, no exclamation marks.

Niche: AI industry insider / model releases / AGI timeline.
Audience: AI builders, traders of AI hype, curious normies. The same audience that follows
model-release leaks, benchmark drama, and "who wins the AGI race" content.

## 2. Content pillars (rotate daily)

1. **The Tease** — short, no claim, maximum curiosity. "she's coming." / "the whale is coming."
2. **The Leak** — model names + numbers + timelines. "someone inside [lab] told me…"
3. **The Prophecy** — AGI, the curve, the singularity. "it's over." / "this year we solve intelligence."
4. **Product verdict** — hot takes on new releases. "wow. i just used @tool…"
5. **Rivalry scorekeeping** — Grok vs GPT vs Gemini. One lab wins, score across posts.
6. **Access hook** — early access, drop-a-link, "raise your hand."
7. **Reply / direct address** — @ the big accounts, react to their news.
8. **Earnest reflection** — rare, human, memorable. "if you are experiencing dark times."
9. **The Lab Notes (daily helpful thread)** — ONE researched, genuinely useful thread per day
   for AI experts: real tools, workflows, techniques ("the lab actually uses this").
   Facts verified before posting — never fabricated. This is the trust pillar that makes
   the leak persona believable and gives followers a reason to stay.

Cadence: 2–3 posts/day to start (aggressive but sustainable). One thread per week (the
"leak thread" longform) PLUS one Lab Notes helpful thread per day. All in strawberry voice.

## 3. Growth mechanics

- **Consistency beats virality.** 2–3 posts/day, same voice, same cadence, for 90 days.
- **Reply to big accounts** in-voice. Reply-guying is the cheapest distribution on X:
  @sama, @OpenAI, @AnthropicAI, @elonmusk, @GoogleDeepMind, @xai — react to their news
  with a one-line strawberry-voice take.
- **Quote-post the news** with a hot take, not a summary.
- **Threads for reach:** the weekly "leak thread" gets retweeted by followers; threads
  outperform single posts for new-account discovery.
- **Engagement bait within ToS:** "raise your hand if…" / "drop your take" / polls on
  "who wins 2026" — no fake engagement, no bought followers, no follow-for-follow.
- **Optimize posting times:** 9am, 1pm, 6pm ET (US tech audience awake). Adjust after
  30 days of analytics.

## 4. Monetization roadmap (honest, phased)

Phase 1 — BUILD (days 1–90): zero revenue target. Goal: 1–5K followers, consistent
engagement, find which pillar resonates. Do NOT monetize early — it kills trust.

Phase 2 — ACTIVATE (months 3–6): once account is credible (5K+ followers, real engagement):
- **X Ads Revenue Sharing** (creator ads): requires X Premium + 500 followers + 5M organic
  impressions in 3 months. Small payouts; turn it on when eligible.
- **Affiliate links:** AI tools (API providers, dev tools, newsletters) with affiliate
  programs — 20–40% recurring on SaaS is the real money. Disclose per platform rules.
- **Digital products:** the voice itself is the product — a paid "AI insider brief"
  newsletter (Beehiiv/Substack), a prompt pack, a "how to think about AGI" ebook.
- **Sponsored posts:** once 10K+ engaged followers, brands pay $100–$500/post for
  native-sounding AI tool mentions. Broker carefully; never shill something the voice
  would hate.

Phase 3 — SCALE (months 6–12):
- **Newsletter as the asset** (owned audience, portable): 2–5K subscribers is worth
  $500–$2K/month in sponsorships alone.
- **Premium tiers:** paid Discord/community for "insider" updates (wait until real
  audience trust exists).
- **Consulting / deal flow:** the account becomes a lead magnet for AI-related services.

## 5. Compliance rails (non-negotiable)

- NO fabricated "leaks" about real people/companies stated as fact — the persona is
  fiction, but posts must not defame real people. When the persona claims something
  specific about a real company, frame as rumor/paraphrase ("i'm hearing", "sources
  say"), never as provable falsehood.
- NO scams, crypto shilling, pump-and-dump, fake giveaways, impersonation of real
  accounts, bought engagement, or affiliate fraud. Those get accounts suspended and
  money clawed back.
- NO spam: no mass-DM, no follow-farming, no engagement pods.
- Affiliate/sponsored content disclosed where platform rules require.
- The voice can be wrong (recanting is in-voice) but never maliciously deceptive.

## 6. KPIs (measured weekly, reviewed monthly)

- Followers (tier targets: 1K by day 60, 5K by day 180)
- Engagement rate per post (target: >2% on 1K+ followers)
- Impressions (target: 5M/3mo for X revenue share eligibility)
- Which pillar wins (tease/leak/prophecy/verdict)
- Best posting time
- Newsletter subscribers (Phase 2+)
